## Publishing policy

- Do not promote a reference-only photo into `assets/vehicles/<model>/<year>/<paint>/<view>.webp` until it **actually is** an approved car-only transparent overlay. Never convert the image to an AI lookalike and call it photographic.
- Retain attribution and the source-page/licence URL for CC BY images. Derivative work from CC BY-SA 4.0 must be distributed under a compatible Share-Alike licence.
- An identical vehicle can have different factory availability by market/model year. The G6 facelift first appeared globally in 2025 but the refreshed model arrived in Israel in late 2025 and is marketed as MY2026. The original `2023-2025` Israeli series and facelift `2026` gallery are deliberately kept separate; overseas MY2025 cars should not be assumed identical.
- Trim labels do not affect any image, only year and paint selection do.
- Before publishing each candidate: photo provenance → model/generation/paint/view verification → transparent artwork → visual comparison → mapping sync → tests → PR review.
