# XPENG Vehicle Card v1.0.9 — Stable

Bug-fix release for the Location map and the Climate page.

## Fixes
- **Location map is centered on the vehicle on every screen.** On wide (16:9) screens the native map was rendered as a fixed 4:3 box and then cropped by the modal, so the car ended up at the bottom edge or out of view. The map now fills the modal area exactly.
- **The car badge itself is centered**, not the tip of its pin, so the vehicle sits in the middle of the map on phone, tablet and desktop.
- **The map follows the vehicle** if it moves while the Location window is open.
- **Climate opens even when the HVAC mode is "unknown".** Xpengtk reports the cabin climate entity as `unknown` while the car has not published a mode; the Climate button used to be disabled. It now opens whenever any climate entity is available, and Climate power shows the real On/Off from the HVAC status sensor (or "Status unknown").

## Card
`dist/xpeng-vehicle-card.js` is identical to `src/xpeng-vehicle-card.js`. No entity mappings, assets or artwork changed.
